# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (2.1, short form)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: showing
empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, giving and gracefully
accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and
focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or
private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission,
and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with them.
Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to **security@stampmitra.in**
(subject line "Code of Conduct"). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1:
